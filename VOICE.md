# v5 voice: lengthen the client-approved script in its own voice

Source of truth: the client-approved Google Docs (BB 29 Sep 2026, PPK). v5 `src_epN.txt` files are copied from them.
Why v5: v4 had no errors but lost the narration flow. v4 padded length with physical description and
"എന്ന് X പറഞ്ഞു" after every quote. The client scripts never do either.

How the client narrates (keep doing this):
1. Storyteller voice. The narrator talks to the listener: "ഇനി ആന്റണിയെക്കുറിച്ച് കുറച്ചു കാര്യങ്ങൾ പറയാം."
2. Thoughts and speech slide into narration without quotes (free indirect):
   "അവിടുത്തെ supervisor ഭയങ്കര ചൂടനാ. ഇവിടെ നിന്ന് സംസാരിക്കുന്നത് കണ്ടാ പിന്നെ പ്രശ്നമാ."
3. The speaker is set up BEFORE the quote with an action or verb and "..": "അവളൊന്ന് കളിയാക്കി.. “…”"
   No "എന്ന് X പറഞ്ഞു" after quotes.
4. Almost no physical description. One fragment at most ("വളർന്ന താടി.. ചുളിഞ്ഞ shirt..").
5. Scene cuts are one short line: "hall-ന്റെ വേറൊരു ഭാഗം." "തിരികെ hall-ൽ." "അതേ സമയം…"
6. Short colloquial sentences (-ആ, -വാ, -ല്ലോ endings).

How to lengthen (585-650 words):
- Keep the client's sentences, in order (aim 90%+).
- Add more of the moment: the next line of the conversation, a character's thought in the narrator's voice,
  a small action that moves the story, a narrator aside. Never a list of what things look like.
- Never spoil a later reveal.
This replaces STYLE.md rules 3 (narrator questions) and 14 (tag every quote) for v5.
